IL-29: AI Discarded a Worker’s Error Output

The worker ran, but its systemd unit discarded standard output and standard error.

I wanted a background worker on RHEL 10 to run as the unprivileged inventorysvc account inside a chroot. Once it was running, an operator would still need its messages to diagnose failures.

An AI agent delivered a systemd unit that discarded both standard output and standard error. The worker could run, but messages sent to those streams would not be retained.

A running worker did not prove its errors were being recorded

Logging-related socket placeholders remained inside the chroot. Their presence did not show a connection to a working collector. The missing check was to emit a message from the actual confined worker and retrieve it at the intended log destination.

The record did not establish that every application-specific log file or host-level observation was absent. It showed that the delivered unit discarded the worker’s normal output and error streams.
