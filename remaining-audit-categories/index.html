When AI Agents Administer Linux: 27 Field Notes

A separate article for each category, covering the different ways the issue appeared in the recorded executions.

What happens when an AI agent administers a Linux server? These field notes follow concrete examples from recorded tests: leaked credentials, firewall changes, failed recovery, incomplete automation and other operational problems.

Each article explains how the issue appeared in different tasks and what the evidence actually establishes. The examples include observed failures, corrected mistakes, implementation risks and missing checks. They are not reports of production intrusions.
